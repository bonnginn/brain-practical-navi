# v1.0.0 learning workspace

The circuit entrance is separate from surface and section observation. The connection graph and existing specimen view share one compact workspace. All available structures belonging to the selected route remain visible; the active stage is emphasized without hiding the other stages. Signal playback is an educational indication of sequence, not a reconstruction of every nerve fibre. Missing models are explained explicitly.

Review remains a single main navigation destination for questions and results. Returning restores the circuit, stage, observation and camera context. Route changes use appropriate framing and keep per-route view state. Narrow windows use compact header controls while keeping observation space usable.

Seven routes have short bilingual role descriptions. Partial segmentation, schematic landmarks, unmodelled structures and pending independent expert validation remain disclosed. No unadopted teacher drafts or later anatomy prototypes enter the student distribution.

Release verification covers build/type checks, current and legacy quiz catalogs, history restoration, responsive circuit behaviour, normal and Pages-base notices, and the existing data/geometry test suite. The statistics Worker accepts the two clarified-label revisions while preserving previous versions. Physical Mac/iPhone full QA remains outside this verification.

## 2026-10-22 usability update

Observation controls use Alt+wheel for zoom so ordinary scrolling can move the page. Section-aligned 3D uses rotations that preserve the anatomical handedness. Answer review keeps the observed question separate from the next question and returns to the same results. Existing representative labelled sections may be offered for observation without changing the question or its answer.

Central-sulcus rendering retains the previous guide implementation. The hidden dashed-line experiment is excluded from this release; full-contour identification and clinical visual-field lessons remain unresolved. Existing scientific, source and adoption limits remain in force.
