Modifications by Brain Practical Navigator, 2026-10-01:
Retrieved six published same-specimen neuroglancer masks, using the actual scale-specific resolutions and the documented voxel-centre convention. The available overview scales are 40 or 80 micrometres. Applied official BigBrain native-linear, native nonlinear and improved ICBM registration. Sampled each region at 0.5 mm application voxel centres and unioned the three regions separately for each hemisphere. Calculated the mean of occupied application voxel centres as a representative position, then converted to the application's centred display coordinates. Native100 original-image alignment was reviewed on 18 planes.

The guide displays the left representative point only; the right position is retained separately and is not generated by mirroring. The dot and dashed circle are orientation aids, not nuclear boundaries or sizes. Source histological mapping included automated delineation and interpolation. This reference is not a new expert validation of the application's coordinates. No MGB voxel labels or auditory radiation segmentation are added to the application segmentation. The guide additionally distributes a left-sided literature reference surface reconstructed from the reviewed 571 occupied app500 samples with marching cubes at level 0.5. No smoothing, filling, component deletion or mirroring was applied. This pink reference surface is kept distinct from the adopted blue LGN segmentation and is not an expert validation or a new application nuclear label.

Metadata, transform hashes and source-record hashes are retained in app/thalamusMgbReference.json, app/thalamusMgbSurface.json and the repository's MGB reference review document. Original images, decoded source masks, chunks and detailed SHA records remain in the local review workspace.
