Connect 3D, serial sections and block specimens

Brain Practical Navigator is a neuroanatomy learning aid that connects where a cut is made with the structures visible at that location. Move between 3D, serial sections and block specimens to relate spatial anatomy to sections. Japanese and English are supported.

Get started

Open the existing teaching app and choose the workspace you want to study. Use its language switch to select Japanese or English.

Educational scope and sources

This is an educational aid, not a tool for clinical decisions or quantitative research. Observed segmentation, schematic teaching models, project adoption and expert validation are distinct states. Model display or passing tests must not be treated as proof of anatomical validity.

The teaching data have source-specific conditions. Review the data, sources and change history and license notices.
