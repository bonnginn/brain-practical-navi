These meshes were procedurally generated from manually placed teaching paths and shapes
in the MNI-oriented display space used by this application. They were not
extracted from BigBrain histology, angiography, tractography, a donor specimen,
or a third-party atlas illustration.

They show major practical landmarks only. Individual variation, small
perforating branches, distal cranial-nerve courses, skull foramina, morphometry,
and surgical relationships are intentionally omitted. They are not validated
anatomical ground truth and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, surgery,
or quantitative research.

The BNM3 mesh files contain project-authored per-vertex structure IDs 1-45.
These IDs only support individual selection and white highlighting in the
teaching interface; they are not atlas labels or validated segmentations.
