Files in this application include browser-ready derivatives of BigBrain
histology and fixed-brain MRI. Changes include grid selection/resampling,
8-bit conversion, compression, background masking and display tone mapping.
The teaching overlays may also include converted manual labels and provisional
image-guided masks. Provisional masks are not anatomical ground truth.
