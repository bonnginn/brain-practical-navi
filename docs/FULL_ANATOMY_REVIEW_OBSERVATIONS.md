## 溝・深部・脳底・神経血管・ブロックの個別観察記録（英語原記録）

## Sulci (8)
- central-sulcus: superior-to-lateral peri-central strip, occasional neighboring crown spill and disconnected patches; location marker, not traced sulcal walls.
- precentral-sulcus: anterior peri-central strip in lateral/top views; broad jagged width crosses crowns at bends, not histological boundary.
- lateral-sulcus: follows frontotemporal cleft, broad patch at anterior end; anterior branches and insular depth not represented.
- superior-frontal-sulcus: frontal longitudinal strip anterior to precentral, coarse crown-crossing; fixed reference ID does not solve natural coloring.
- calcarine-sulcus: short medial occipital trace between cuneus/lingual region, mostly obscured from lateral surface as expected; full banks not represented.
- parieto-occipital-sulcus: medial diagonal notch toward calcarine region, small superior lateral extension; no gross displaced lobe, exact depth unverified.
- longitudinal-fissure: floating white arched tube over midline, clearly schematic path rather than cortical segmentation.
- olfactory-sulcus: paired basal anterior frontal patches beside olfactory pathway, discontinuous broad patches do not outline the full groove.

## Deep and buried follow-up
All five buried cortical ghost sheets and all five deep ghost sheets viewed. Transverse temporal gyri: paired oblique supratemporal buried patches now visible. Insula: bilateral deep lateral cortical sheets visible under opercula. Parahippocampal: medial temporal undersurface patch. Fusiform: elongated ventral bilateral ribbon. Lingual: medial posterior ventral patches. No gross item swap in these revealed views; atlas partition boundaries not independently traced.
- corpus-callosum: ghost view shows sagittal arch but unnaturally straight lateral walls and extra inner arc; confirms ID30 extent/source warning, not genuine whole callosal fibers.
- fornix: two uniform parallel bent tubes under callosum; descending anterior turn visible, posterior crural spread/fimbria and commissure not anatomically reconstructed. Schematic only.
- septum-pellucidum: flat midline triangular plate below callosum, positional aid not two histological leaves.
- thalami: paired paramedian masses above hypothalamic marker, no gross spatial swap; subnuclear borders absent.
