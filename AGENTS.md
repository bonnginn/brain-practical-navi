# Brain Practical Navigator

Japanese/English neuroanatomy teaching app; not for clinical decisions or quantitative research. Preserve these project-specific boundaries:

- Keep observed segmentation, schematic teaching models, project adoption and expert validation distinct. Model capability or passing tests does not establish anatomical validity.
- Preserve raw images, review figures and reversible evidence in `work/`, fixtures and segmentation patches. Do not rewrite hash-pinned historical evidence to tidy documentation.
- Respect donor dignity, privacy, source-specific licences and the project's non-official educational status. Do not put patient data, unlicensed specimen/textbook images, or private form/edit/response links in public assets or PRs.
- Local development and public releases may contain different labels. Preserve both; do not overwrite development with main or publish unrelated work. Push, main integration and deployment require user authorization covering that scope; an existing explicit instruction is sufficient.
- Current user instructions determine scope, deadlines and delegation. Dated stop notices, model assignments and approval records are history, not standing authorization or a new reason to stop an authorized task.
